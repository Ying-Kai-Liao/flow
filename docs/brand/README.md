# flow brand

The flow mark is an f drawn as three parallel lanes. Each lane is a worker in its own worktree. One green crossbar
runs through all three lanes: that's you, the one decision that spans every lane.

## 2. Clear space
Leave **1 × the height of the wordmark's "o"** clear on every side of a lockup. For the symbol on its own, leave
**¼ of the symbol's height**. The clear space scales with the logo.

## 3. Minimum size
| Version | Screen | Print |
|---|---|---|
| Horizontal lockup | 96 px wide | 25 mm wide |
| Stacked lockup | 64 px wide | 18 mm wide |
| Symbol (master) | 48 px | 12 mm |
| Symbol (small cut, `flow-symbol-small.svg`) | 16–47 px | 5–11 mm |

Below 48 px, always use the small cut. Its lanes are heavier and its gaps wider, so they stay open at 16 px.

## 4. Colour
| Name | HEX | RGB | CMYK (approx.) | Pantone (closest) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ink | `#15171C` | 21 23 28 | 25 18 0 89 | Black 6 C |
| Decision green | `#0B9A68` | 11 154 104 | 93 0 32 40 | 340 C |
| Decision green, on dark | `#2FD18F` | 47 209 143 | 78 0 32 18 | 7480 C |

Use green **only on the crossbar**. Everything else is ink, or white on dark backgrounds.

Contrast: ink on white is 17.9:1. Green on white is 3.6:1, which passes for graphics (3:1) but is too low for body
text. The dark-mode green on ink is 9.1:1.

**Approved logo and background pairs:**
- two-colour on white or light grey
- reversed (white lanes, `#2FD18F` bar) on ink
- one-colour black on white
- one-colour white on ink or on green

## 5. Typography
The wordmark is custom-drawn: a monoline with a 16-unit stroke and rounded terminals. Don't type it out. For supporting
text, use a neutral grotesque such as Inter (OFL) or the system UI stack, and keep all text lowercase "flow".

## 6. Don'ts
- Don't stretch, rotate or skew the logo.
- Don't colour the lanes green, and don't move the crossbar.
- Don't add shadows, outlines or gradients.
- Don't change the spacing or size relationship between symbol and wordmark.
- Don't use the master symbol below 48 px.
- Don't place the logo on busy backgrounds without an ink tile.

## 7. Notes
- A trademark search hasn't been done. Search "flow" marks in software classes (USPTO/EUIPO, class 9 and 42) before
  any public launch.
